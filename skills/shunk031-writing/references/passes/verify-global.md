# Verify global structure

Try to refute the draft against the contract. Check whether the main point answers the reader's need, the skeleton has a necessary job for every section, the development is proportionate to those jobs, and lists or groups are genuinely parallel. Flag piled-up additions that patch sentences without rebuilding the structure. Flag any section or paragraph that repeats a claim or reader need without adding evidence or a consequence.

Specimen: "Does the paper have a central thesis?"
