# Verify global structure

Try to refute the draft against the contract. Check whether the main point answers the reader's need, the skeleton has a necessary job for every section, the development is proportionate to those jobs, and lists or groups are genuinely parallel. Flag piled-up additions that patch sentences without rebuilding the structure. Flag any section or paragraph that repeats a claim or reader need without adding evidence or a consequence.

Run the 30-second-skim test: headings, opening sentences, and tables should convey the answer and top caveats. Check that major sections open with their takeaway, headings use sentence case without manual section numbers, and the format matches the content. Decisions, steps, and labeled-link runs should use a lead-in and parallel bullets; short atomic-token references should stay inline, while exact enumerations that carry the claim should remain explicit. In tables, use `<br>` for separate lines in cells and align text left and purely numeric values right.

Specimen: "Does the paper have a central thesis?"
