# Research paper

Build paragraph-level topic sentences before prose. Use this seven-part skeleton: Abstract, Introduction, Related Work, Method, Experiments, Discussion, and Conclusion. Keep observed results in Experiments and interpretation in Discussion.

Owner-authored specimen: 「トピックセンテンスを読むだけで論文の全体がざっくりと理解できます。」
